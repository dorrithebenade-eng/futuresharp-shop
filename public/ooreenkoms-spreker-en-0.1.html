This agreement is between Future Sharp and the speaker who signs it. It applies to the speaker and not to individual talks: every talk he subsequently offers on FutureSharp Talks falls under this same agreement.

1. Parties

Future Sharp (registration number 2024/117444/08), with address at P O Box 11602, Queenswood, Pretoria, Gauteng, 0121, hereinafter "Future Sharp".

The speaker, hereinafter "the speaker". The speaker's identification and banking details are supplied on registration and form part of this agreement.

2. Purpose of this agreement

The speaker grants Future Sharp the right to offer and sell the video recording of his talk on FutureSharp Talks, part of Future Shop. Future Sharp operates the platform, hosts and streams the recording, and processes the payments; the speaker remains the owner of his talk at all times.

This agreement is not a production or publishing contract. Future Sharp acquires no ownership of the talk.

3. Copyright and licence

  • The speaker retains full copyright in his talk, including its content, ideas and wording.
  • The speaker grants Future Sharp a licence to host, stream, display and sell the recording on FutureSharp Talks for the duration of this agreement, and thereafter only to the extent set out in clause 9.
  • The full recording offered on FutureSharp Talks may not be made freely available elsewhere, for example on a public video channel or website, while this agreement is in force. The speaker remains free to give the same talk live, to write and publish about the same ideas, and to use its content in his own teaching and practice.
  • Either party may use an excerpt of the recording of up to 60 seconds, together with the title, the speaker's name and photograph and the cover image, to promote the talk and FutureSharp Talks, provided that the excerpt refers to the talk on FutureSharp Talks.
  • The speaker warrants that he is entitled to offer the talk, that it infringes no other party's copyright, and that he holds the necessary permission for any music, images, video or other material in the recording, and from any identifiable person who appears in it.

Recordings made by Future Sharp

Where Future Sharp records the talk, the speaker retains copyright in the talk and Future Sharp retains the recording it made. Future Sharp uses that recording only as set out in this agreement.

4. The recording

A talk is a single video recording of about fifteen minutes. It is offered on its own page on FutureSharp Talks, with a title, a summary, keywords and one or more categories.

A buyer watches the talk in his Theatre on Future Shop. The recording is streamed and cannot be downloaded, and each viewing link expires after a few hours.

The speaker supplies the recording, or a direct download link to it, in a usable quality, or attends a recording arranged by Future Sharp.

5. Price and division

The speaker usually receives 70% of the selling price, and Future Sharp retains 30%. The exact division is determined per talk and may differ by agreement.

The selling price and the division for each talk are confirmed in writing between the parties before that talk is offered for sale. No price or division is thereafter changed without the speaker's knowledge.

Where more than one speaker presents a talk, the speakers' share is divided among them according to the percentages they have agreed between themselves. Each such speaker concludes his own agreement with Future Sharp.

6. Payment

  • The speaker's share is divided at the point of sale and paid into the bank account he supplied on registration. Future Sharp holds no funds on the speaker's behalf.
  • For this purpose a sub-account is created with the payment service in the speaker's name. The first payout to a new sub-account may be held by the service until it has been verified.
  • On registration the speaker supplies a bank letter not older than three months, and a copy of his identity document or passport. The name on the bank letter must match the account holder.
  • The speaker has continuous access, in his speaker panel, to a report of the visits to his talk pages, his sales and his share.
  • A refund to a buyer, or a reversed payment, is recovered from the division of the sale concerned. A talk given free of charge through a coupon code carries no division.

7. Responsibilities of the speaker

  • To supply the recording, or to attend a recording, as set out in clause 4.
  • To supply the title, summary and keywords of each talk, and to choose its categories in consultation with Future Sharp.
  • To supply a cover image, or to confirm that Future Sharp may create one.
  • To determine the price of each talk in consultation with Future Sharp.
  • To supply correct banking details and to notify Future Sharp when they change.
  • To notify Future Sharp if the content of a talk is withdrawn, corrected or superseded.

Professional content

A talk shares knowledge and ideas and is not professional advice to any individual viewer. Where a talk deals with medicine, law, finance or another regulated field, the speaker takes responsibility for the accuracy of what he presents and, where appropriate, says so in the talk.

8. Coupon codes

The speaker receives three coupon codes per talk for his own use, for example for colleagues, promotion or gifts. Additional codes may be requested with motivation.

9. Duration and termination

  • This agreement takes effect on signature and continues until one of the parties terminates it.
  • Either party may terminate the agreement on 30 days' written notice.
  • The speaker may withdraw an individual talk without terminating the agreement in its entirety.

Buyers who have already purchased

  • Termination does not affect buyers who have already paid.
  • A buyer who has purchased a talk permanently retains access to it, and Future Sharp may continue to host and stream the recording to those buyers for that purpose only. This access does not lapse on termination of the agreement.
  • New sales cease on the date on which the termination takes effect.
  • Divisions on sales that took place before that date are still paid in terms of this agreement.

Where a talk is withdrawn owing to a claim by a third party, Future Sharp may remove it from sale immediately while the matter is resolved.

10. Data and privacy

Future Sharp collects buyer data for the purposes of the transaction. The speaker receives the number of visits to his talk pages, the number of buyers and his share. He receives no personal information about buyers.

The speaker's own details are used for payment, contact and reporting, and are not shared for any other purpose.

11. Liability

Future Sharp takes reasonable care to keep the platform available and secure, but does not warrant uninterrupted operation. Future Sharp is not liable for loss arising from an interruption at a third party, including the video and payment services.

The speaker indemnifies Future Sharp against any claim arising from the content of his talk, including copyright claims, defamation, the use of material or people without permission, and the infringement of a third party's rights.

12. Amendments

Amendments to this agreement must be in writing and signed by both parties. The addition of a new talk is not an amendment and requires only written confirmation of the price and the division by both parties.

The speaker updates his contact details himself. A change to the banking details is requested by the speaker and takes effect when Future Sharp confirms it; this is not an amendment to this agreement.

13. Applicable law

This agreement is governed by the law of the Republic of South Africa. The parties undertake to attempt to resolve any dispute between themselves in good faith before referring it to a court.

14. Signature

The speaker signs this agreement electronically by typing his full name on registration and ticking the confirmation alongside it. The name, the date and the version of this agreement are recorded against his record.

Future Sharp accepts the agreement when the speaker's registration is confirmed. Both parties thereafter receive a copy bearing both signatures and dates.

By signing, both parties confirm that they have read and understood this agreement.
