This agreement is between Future Sharp and the author who signs it. It applies to the author and not to individual titles: every work he subsequently publishes on Future Shop falls under this same agreement.

1. Parties

Future Sharp (registration number 2024/117444/08), with address at P O Box 11602, Queenswood, Pretoria, Gauteng, 0121, hereinafter "Future Sharp".

The author, hereinafter "the author". The author's identification and banking details are supplied on registration and form part of this agreement.

2. Purpose of this agreement

The author grants Future Sharp the right to offer and sell his work on Future Shop. Future Sharp operates the platform and processes the payments; the author remains the owner of his work at all times.

This agreement is not a publishing contract. Future Sharp acquires no rights in the work and takes no ownership of it.

3. Copyright and ownership

  • The author retains full copyright in his work.
  • The author grants Future Sharp a non-exclusive right to display, distribute and sell the work on Future Shop for the duration of this agreement.
  • Non-exclusive means that the author may also sell or publish the same work elsewhere. Future Sharp requires no exclusivity.
  • The author warrants that he is entitled to publish the work, and that it infringes no other party's copyright.

4. Formats

A title may be offered in three formats, each at its own price:

  • E-book — read in Future Shop's own reader.
  • Hard copy — a printed copy, supplied and dispatched by the author.
  • Loan — temporary access to the same e-book, for a fixed period.

The author decides for himself which formats he offers for each title.

5. Price and division

The author usually receives 70% of the selling price, and Future Sharp retains 30%. The exact division is determined per title and per format and may differ by agreement.

The selling price and the division for each title are confirmed in writing between the parties before that title is offered for sale. No price or division is thereafter changed without the author's knowledge.

Where more than one author has contributed to a title, the author's share is divided among them according to the percentages they have agreed between themselves. Each such author concludes his own agreement with Future Sharp.

6. Payment

  • The author's share is divided at the point of sale and paid into the bank account he supplied on registration. Future Sharp holds no funds on the author's behalf.
  • For this purpose a sub-account is created with the payment service in the author's name. The first payout to a new sub-account may be held by the service until it has been verified.
  • On registration the author supplies a bank letter not older than three months, and a copy of his identity document or passport. The name on the bank letter must match the account holder.
  • The author has continuous access to a report of his sales and views.
  • A refund to a buyer, or a reversed payment, is recovered from the division of the sale concerned.

7. Responsibilities of the author

  • To supply the manuscript in a usable format, in accordance with Future Shop's guidelines.
  • To supply the cover image, or to confirm that Future Sharp may design one.
  • To determine the price of each title in consultation with Future Sharp.
  • To supply correct banking details and to notify Future Sharp when they change.
  • To notify Future Sharp if the work is withdrawn or amended elsewhere.

Printed copies

Where a title is offered in hard copy, the printing and the delivery are the author's responsibility. Future Sharp sells the copy, takes the payment, and passes the order to the author together with the buyer's delivery details.

The author undertakes to fulfil each such order within the delivery time he has stated. The cost of printing and dispatch comes out of his own share.

ISBN

Future Shop does not issue ISBNs. Where the author holds one, it is displayed on the page of the title concerned. Applying for an ISBN and the legal deposit of the work remain the author's responsibility.

8. Coupon codes

The author receives three coupon codes per title for his own use, for example for reviewers, promotion or gifts. Additional codes may be requested with motivation.

9. Duration and termination

This agreement takes effect on signature and continues until one of the parties terminates it.

  • Either party may terminate the agreement on 30 days' written notice.
  • The author may withdraw an individual title without terminating the agreement in its entirety.

Buyers who have already purchased

Termination does not affect buyers who have already paid.

  • A buyer who has purchased an e-book permanently retains access to it. This access does not lapse on termination of the agreement.
  • An active loan runs its full period.
  • A printed copy already ordered is delivered by the author.
  • New sales cease on the date on which the termination takes effect.
  • Divisions on sales that took place before that date are still paid in terms of this agreement.

Where a work is withdrawn owing to a copyright claim by a third party, Future Sharp may remove it from the shop immediately while the matter is resolved.

10. Data and privacy

Future Sharp collects buyer data for the purposes of the transaction and the delivery. The author receives sales figures and views, and — where he must deliver a printed copy — the delivery details of the buyer concerned. He receives no other personal information about buyers.

The author's own details are used for payment, contact and reporting, and are not shared for any other purpose.

11. Liability

Future Sharp takes reasonable care to keep the platform available and secure, but does not warrant uninterrupted operation. Future Sharp is not liable for loss arising from an interruption at a third party.

The author indemnifies Future Sharp against any claim arising from the content of his work, including copyright claims, defamation and the infringement of a third party's rights.

12. Amendments

Amendments to this agreement must be in writing and signed by both parties. The addition of a new title is not an amendment and requires only written confirmation of the price and the division by both parties.

The author updates his contact details himself in his author panel. A change to the banking details is requested by the author in his panel and takes effect when Future Sharp confirms it; this is not an amendment to this agreement.

13. Applicable law

This agreement is governed by the law of the Republic of South Africa. The parties undertake to attempt to resolve any dispute between themselves in good faith before referring it to a court.

14. Signature

The author signs this agreement electronically by typing his full name on registration and ticking the confirmation alongside it. The name, the date and the version of this agreement are recorded against his record.

Future Sharp accepts the agreement when the author's registration is confirmed. Both parties thereafter receive a copy bearing both signatures and dates.

By signing, both parties confirm that they have read and understood this agreement.
